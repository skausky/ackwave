Network to watch

Pick your own router. The board only listens and never joins, so no password is needed. It is 2.4 GHz only. The choice is saved on the board, which restarts and recalibrates (about 15 s).

The motion score is each link's movement energy as a multiple of the room's quiet level. Motion starts when the short (0.5 s) score crosses its threshold while the long (4 s) one is up too, or when the long one crosses its own. Shaded red: motion. Grey bands: router background scans, which the board ignores. Ticks along the bottom: the board's own verdict changing.
