Router ACKs: shape jump mean change per subcarrier (0–1)

How far each packet's shape is from the running reference. Over the glitch limit (red) a packet is ignored unless the new shape lasts 5 s; near a router scan the stricter limit (orange) applies.

Router ACKs: gap before each packet milliseconds

Time since the previous ACK on that link. Over 100 ms (and 4× the usual) marks a router scan. Gaps of 60 ms or more also make brief blips much likelier.

Network

NetworkChSignal

Pick your own router. The board only listens and never joins, so no password is needed. It is 2.4 GHz only. The choice is saved on the board, which restarts and recalibrates (about 15 s).

Detection

Sensitivity is the one threshold the board lets you change (the slow, 4 s window). The rest are shown for reference; changing them needs new firmware.

Buzzer

Beeps: "di-di-DIIING" once motion has lasted 2 s (at most every 30 s), a sliding "dooooo" when it ends. Alarm: a siren while motion lasts (always loud). While this dashboard runs it keeps the board at these settings, even after a restart.

Move the mouse (or a finger) over any chart to read the time and values there.

The motion score is each link's movement energy as a multiple of the room's quiet level. Motion starts when the short (0.5 s) score crosses its threshold while the long (4 s) one is up too, or when the long one crosses its own. The verdict, the counts and "last motion" are the board's own.
