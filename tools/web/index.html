      Tick the device links that should also switch the light, the buzzer and the board's LED (purple for motion only a device link sees).       Where the board senses motion

      Live links

      How to read this

      A link's motion zone depends only on its length and the channel, so once the dots are where things really are, the ovals are exact. Motion right next to the board or the device counts too, even outside the oval, because that end of the link is so close.

      The dashed ring around a dot means it was placed from its signal strength, which is rough: walls and furniture make things look farther away. Drag it to its real place.

      The "open air" estimate uses the free-space loss and the environment's fade rate. A link that is many dB weaker than that has walls or furniture in between.

            Several boards

            When the house counts as "motion" for the light, the alerts and the board's LED and buzzer. A brief blip from one board is often noise; asking a second board to confirm it cuts most of those without losing longer motion.

            Network

            NetworkChSignal

            Pick your own router: Watch uses every access point with that name on the board's channel. To combine several routers (any names), tick their access points instead. The board listens on one channel, so set those routers to the same 2.4 GHz channel in their own settings. The board only listens and never joins, so no password is needed. The choice is saved on the board, which restarts and recalibrates (about 15 s).

            Detection

            Sensitivity is the one threshold the board lets you change (the slow, 4 s window); it is saved on the board. The rest are shown for reference; changing them needs new firmware.

            Buzzer

            Beeps: "di-di-DIIING" once motion has lasted 2 s (at most every 30 s), a sliding "dooooo" when it ends. Alarm: a siren while motion lasts. Every sound uses the volume set here. Saved on the board, so it keeps them unplugged from the PC too; hold BOOT for 5 s to put the board's settings back to the firmware's.

            Board LED

            Motion looks

            Status shows everything: motion in your colour, amber fading for 30 s after it, a quick purple flash for a blip too faint to count, a slow blue pulse while calibrating, yellow while looking for the router, and dark when the room is still. Motion only lights it for motion and nothing else. Saved on the board, so it stays the same when the board runs on its own.

            Light or smart device on motion

            Calls the first URL the moment motion starts and the second once the room has been still for the time above ("right away" switches it off as soon as the motion ends; brief blips then flick it on and off, and Voice Monkey may slow down very frequent calls). It only switches off a light it switched on. For an Amazon Smart Plug, make two Routine Trigger devices in Voice Monkey (e.g. "ackwave motion" and "ackwave still"), then two Alexa Routines: when each one is pressed, turn the plug on / off. Paste each device's trigger URL here. Any URL that switches something works (Home Assistant, IFTTT webhooks). The URLs hold secret tokens: they are kept in sessions/dashboard.json on this PC and never shown again.
